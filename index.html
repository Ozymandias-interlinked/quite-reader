Voice

Human-sounding voices that work offline. A one-time download of about 90 MB, saved on this device.

Built into your phone or computer. Fastest option, with no waiting. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, free Premium voices (such as Ava or Zoe) sound very natural: add them in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices. Some voices in Chrome and Edge need an internet connection.
